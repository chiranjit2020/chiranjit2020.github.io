Design System

The visual foundation for the CODEWORM portfolio. A two-tone brand palette with a dark/light switch, a Manrope + Oranienbaum type scale, restrained spacing, structural borders, and motion tokens.

Color

Two brand anchors — amber and deep green — power both theme modes. Every other tone is a mechanical blend of whichever two are active, so it adapts automatically when you toggle the theme in the header.
